Welcome to Our Sanctuary

Vintage Charm & Modern Delights

Nestled in the heart of Locksbottom, The Drawing Room is a charming haven that radiates warmth and elegant simplicity. Upon stepping inside, you are greeted by cosy vintage decor, fresh blooms, and beautiful mismatched teacups that set the stage for an unforgettable visit.

Our chef and our dedicated team are committed to delivering heartwarming, traditional English cuisine infused with quality and flair. From our hearty lunches to the delicate cakes & treats, crafted with care and served with love.

Our House Specialities

The Drawing Room experiences are quintessentially handcrafted daily and designed to delight your senses.

Elegant Tradition

Afternoon Tea

Delicate finger sandwiches, freshly baked warm scones served with Cornish clotted cream, and an exquisite tier of cakes & treats, baked fresh by our baker, paired with your choice of tea.

Savoury Classic

Signature Hot Salt Beef

Our chef's melt-in-the-mouth salt beef, stacked high on freshly cut, thick-sliced farmhouse bread. Accompanied by traditional Gherkins and hot English mustard.

Baked Fresh

Victoria Sponge

Our baker's masterfully light, double-layered sponge, filled generously with rich vanilla buttercream and deep, sweet home-style strawberry preserve.
